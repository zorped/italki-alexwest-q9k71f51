Script · listen

Your scene, line by line

Danny is on the sofa. The inspector's voice is tinny through the phone. Spoken with pauses, it is about 40 seconds.

  1. Danny

    Inspector saab. Kahiye.

    Inspector, sir. Go ahead.

  2. Inspector

    Aapke logon ne ek ladke ko maar diya.

    Your people killed a boy.

  3. Danny

    Ji. Suna maine.

    Yes. I heard.

  4. Inspector

    Woh paise nahin de paaya.

    He could not pay the money.

  5. Danny

    Heere hote, toh court jaate. Cocaine hai. Kahaan jaayein? Aapke paas?

    If it were diamonds, we would go to court. It is cocaine. Where do we go? To you?

  6. Inspector

    Toh maar doge?

    So you will kill?

  7. Danny

    Aapke paas kaanoon hai. Hamaare paas darr.

    You have the law. We have fear.

  8. Inspector

    Yeh safaai hai?

    Is this a clean-up?

  9. Danny

    Nahin. Hisaab hai. Darr khatam, paisa khatam.

    No. It is business. When the fear ends, the money ends.

  10. Inspector

    Woh kisi ka beta tha.

    He was somebody's son.

  11. Danny

    Baaki ladke bhi kisi ke bete hain. Ab waqt par paise denge.

    The other boys are somebody's sons too. Now they will pay on time.

Three things already in your linesSuna maine puts the verb first, which sounds short and cool. The plain order is Maine suna, as in Lesson 02. Maar diya uses the long aa from Lesson 06, and diya says it is done, as in bata diya. Aapke paas … hai is the same pattern as mere paas in Lesson 06.

Patterns · quiz

Four patterns inside it

Each one comes from a line you wrote. The other examples are for you to borrow.

hote, toh … jaate

what you would do if something were true

  • Heere hote, toh court jaate.
    If it were diamonds, we would go to court.
  • Mujhe pata hota, toh main aata.
    If I had known, I would have come.
  • Waqt hota, toh main aapse milta.
    If I had time, I would meet you.

nahin … paaya

tried, and did not manage

  • Woh paise nahin de paaya.
    He could not pay the money.
  • Main kal shooting par nahin pahunch paaya.
    I could not reach the shoot yesterday.

… khatam, … khatam

two short halves, the first causes the second

  • Darr khatam, paisa khatam.
    When the fear ends, the money ends.
  • Battery khatam, baat khatam.
    The battery is gone, so the call is gone.

koi, but kisi ka

koi changes before a small word

  • Woh kisi ka beta tha.
    He was somebody's son.
  • Maine kisi se baat nahin ki.
    I did not talk to anybody.

Paaya, a boy or a girlPaaya follows the person who could not do it: ladka nahin de paaya, ladki nahin de paayi. Nahin paaya says the person tried. Nahin diya says the person chose not to.

Choice 1: doge or denge?The inspector says aapke logon, which is aap, and then maar doge, which is tum. Aap ends the future in -enge and tum ends it in -oge. Keep doge if he should suddenly sound hard. Change it to maar denge if he should stay polite and cold.

Choice 2: paisa or paise?Paise is the money you hand over, as in paise nahin de paaya. Paisa is money as an idea, so it fits a saying: Darr khatam, paisa khatam. Your line already does this, so the only question is the title.

Check yourself

  1. Mujhe pata    , toh main aata.

  2. Woh ladki kal nahin aa    .

  3. Aap kab    ?

  4. Woh     ka beta tha.

3

Words

Words from the scene

Six words you chose. Each has an example in a different situation.

  • kahiye

    the polite way to say go ahead, tell me, with aap

    Ji, kahiye. Main aapki kya madad kar sakta hoon? Yes, tell me. How can I help you?

  • kaanoon

    the law, which courts and police enforce

    Kaanoon sab ke liye ek hai. The law is the same for everyone.

  • hisaab

    an account of what is owed and what is paid

    Mera hisaab barabar hai. My account is even. I owe nothing.

  • safaai

    cleaning. For a problem, it means clearing it away

    Ghar ki safaai kal hogi. The house will be cleaned tomorrow.

  • waqt par

    at the time it is due, on time. Many people say it close to wakt

    Train waqt par aayi. The train came on time.

  • ladka · beta

    ladka is any boy. Beta is someone's son

    Mera beta paanch saal ka hai. My son is five years old.

4

Speaking

Say it in Hindi

Say your own answer first. The hidden versions are models, not scripts you have to copy.

  1. 1

    Make your own pair, one thing they have and one thing you have. Start with Aapke paas, then Hamaare paas.

    Compare with an example answer

    Aapke paas paise hain. Hamaare paas mehnat hai.

  2. 2

    Say what you would have done if you had known. Use hota, toh.

    Compare with an example answer

    Mujhe pata hota, toh main pehle phone karta.

  3. 3

    Say that you could not do something yesterday, even though you tried. Use nahin … paaya.

    Compare with an example answer

    Main kal meeting mein nahin pahunch paaya.

  4. 4

    Make your own two-halves line about something that ran out. Use khatam twice.

    Compare with an example answer

    Net khatam, kaam khatam.
