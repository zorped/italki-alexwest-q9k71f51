Rule · quiz

Main or maine?

Listen for this when you speak. The small word at the start decides the ending of the sentence.

main … hoon

now, and habits

  • Main roz subah chai peeta hoon.
    I drink tea every morning.
  • Main roz Hindi bolta hoon.
    I speak Hindi every day.

maine … hai / tha

finished action with an object

  • Maine kal ek film dekhi.
    I watched a film yesterday.
  • Maine usko phone kiya tha.
    I called him.

main … gaya / aaya

going and coming have no object, so main stays

  • Main kal Delhi gaya.
    I went to Delhi yesterday.
  • Main samay par pahunch gaya.
    I arrived on time.

Your own sentence, said smoothly

  • Main ek English actor hoon aur maine kaafi saari Bollywood aur Hindi films mein kaam kiya hai.

    I am an English actor and I have worked in quite a lot of Bollywood and Hindi films.

Check yourself

  2.     roz Hindi bolta hoon.

  3.     pichhle saal Delhi gaya tha.

  4. Maine is film mein kaam kiya    .

  5. Main roz subah chai peeta    .

Two pairs that change the meaning

Both pairs are easy to mix up, and each changes what the other person understands.

bata diya
you passed the information on, and it is doneMaine usko meeting ka time bata diya.
bola
you said something to himMaine usko bola ki jaldi aana.
apna
points back to the person doing the action: his own, her own, my ownWoh apne dost ke saath gaya. (his own friend)
uska
belongs to someone elseWoh uske dost ke saath gaya. (another person's friend)

Apna changes with the friendApna agrees with the thing it describes: apne dost for a male friend, apni dost for a female friend.

Is ghus jaana rude?No. Ghus jaana is a normal way to say you get into a house, a crowd or a character. It only sounds crude when the situation itself is sexual, so use it the way it appears in your sentences.

Check yourself

  1. Rohit     dost ke saath gaya. (his own friend)

  2. Rohit     dost ke saath gaya. (someone else's friend)

  3. Maine usko meeting ka time    . (I let him know)

Patterns · quiz

Say what you really want

You built these sentences yourself in class. Here is how they work, and why the last one ends in mile and not milega.

mann karna
the everyday way to say you really want, or feel like doing, somethingMujhe jaanne ka bahut mann karta hai.
chaahna
the verb to want, and the safe choiceMain yeh role karna chahta hoon.
ichchha
a wish, and a more formal wordMeri ichchha hai ki…
chaah
a want or desire as a nounMujhe is kaam ki chaah hai.

Two ways to build it

  • Mujhe jaanne ka bahut mann karta hai.

    I really want to know.

  • Mujhe is film mein kaam karne ka bahut mann karta hai.

    I really want to work in this film.

  • Mera bahut mann karta hai ki main is film mein kaam karoon.

    I really want to work in this film. (a second way)

  • Mera bahut mann karta hai ki mujhe is film mein kaam karne ka mauka mile.

    I really hope I get a chance to work in this film.

Mile or milega?Mujhe mauka milega is a statement: I will get a chance. Mujhe mauka mile is a wish: I hope I get one. After mann karta hai ki, the verb takes this wish form: main karoon, main miloon, mujhe mile.

The same form elsewhereZaroori hai ki means it is important that. Only the opening changes between the two sentences below. The verb stays the same: woh aaye. The wish form is not only for wishes. It follows anything you want or need to happen.

  • Main chahta hoon ki woh jaldi aaye.

    I want him to come soon.

  • Zaroori hai ki woh jaldi aaye.

    It is important that he comes soon.

Check yourself

  1. Mujhe yeh film dekhne     bahut mann karta hai.

  2. Mera mann karta hai ki main kal usse    .

  3. Mera bahut mann karta hai ki mujhe mauka    .

4

Speaking

Say it in Hindi

Say your own answer first. The hidden versions are models, not scripts you have to copy.

  1. 1

    Say two things you did yesterday. Use maine with one, and main with a verb of going.

    Compare with an example answer

    Kal maine ek film dekhi aur phir main ghar chala gaya.

  2. 2

    Say what you really want to do this year. Use both ways of building mann karta hai.

    Compare with an example answer

    Mera bahut mann karta hai ki main is saal ek badi Hindi film mein kaam karoon. Mujhe naye directors se milne ka bhi bahut mann karta hai.

  3. 3

    Say that you passed on some information, and that you also said something to the same person.

    Compare with an example answer

    Maine usko meeting ka time bata diya aur bola ki jaldi aana.

  4. 4

    Say what you want someone to do. Then say that it is important that they do it. Use chahta hoon ki and zaroori hai ki, and keep the verb ending the same.

    Compare with an example answer

    Main chahta hoon ki woh jaldi aaye. Zaroori hai ki woh aaj hi aaye.
