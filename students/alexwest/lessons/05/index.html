Showing real interest, and saying who you have worked with

1

Phrases

Show interest, truthfully

You cannot say you love a film you have not seen. These lines show interest and respect, and every one is true.

  • Mujhe aapke kaam ke baare mein aur jaanna hai.

    I would like to know more about your work.

  • Aapki kaun si film mujhe pehle dekhni chahiye?

    Which of your films should I watch first?

  • Aapne yeh kaam kaise shuru kiya?

    How did you start this work?

  • Aapko apne kaam mein sabse zyaada kya pasand hai?

    What do you like most about your work?

  • Maine abhi aapki saari films nahin dekhi hain, lekin aapke kaam ke baare mein bahut suna hai.

    I have not seen all your films yet, but I have heard a lot about your work. (when they ask which film you liked)

Your thoughts about artists, with more ways to say them

dilchasp lagta · lagti · lagte

the ending follows the word you find interesting

  • Mujhe kalakaar ka maksad bahut dilchasp lagta hai.
    I find what drives an artist very interesting.
  • Mujhe unki soch bahut dilchasp lagti hai.
    I find their way of thinking very interesting.
  • Mujhe is tarah ke log bahut dilchasp lagte hain.
    I find people like this very interesting.

chahte hain ki · wish form

after chahte hain ki, the verb takes the wish form (Lesson 02)

  • Saare kalakaar chahte hain ki log unhein dekhein aur samjhein.
    All artists want people to see them and understand them.
  • Har kalakaar chahta hai ki uska kaam logon tak pahunche.
    Every artist wants his work to reach people.
  • Kalakaar chahte hain ki log unki kadar karein.
    Artists want people to value them.

apne baare mein · apne kaam ke baare mein

batana means to tell, baat karna means to talk

  • Kalakaar ko bhi apne baare mein batana bahut pasand hai.
    Artists also like telling people about themselves.
  • Zyaadatar log apne kaam ke baare mein baat karna pasand karte hain.
    Most people like talking about their work.

karte rehna · to keep doing

rukawat means obstacle

  • Aap is tarah ka kaam kyun karte rehte hain?
    Why do you keep doing this kind of work? (ask once you have been talking a while)
  • Is kaam mein bahut rukawatein hain, phir bhi main karta rehta hoon.
    There are many obstacles in this work, and I still keep doing it.
  • Main jaanna chahta hoon ki aap is tarah ka kaam kyun karte hain.
    I want to know why you do this kind of work.
  • Mushkil hai, lekin main koshish karta rehta hoon.
    It is hard, but I keep trying.

Actor or kalakaarIn interviews and the news, people mostly say actor. Kalakaar means artist. Your sentence: Bahut se actors kalakaar hain, lekin saare actors kalakaar nahin hain.

2

Phrases

Who you have worked with

Name the person and the film, then add one more thing.

ke saath · with

the person comes first, then ke saath

  • Maine Diljit Dosanjh ke saath Sajjan Singh Rangroot mein kaam kiya hai.
    I worked with Diljit Dosanjh in Sajjan Singh Rangroot.
  • Maine Sharad Kelkar aur Yami Gautam ke saath Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga mein kaam kiya hai.
    I worked with Sharad Kelkar and Yami Gautam in Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga.
  • Maine unke saath shooting ki hai.
    I have shot with them.

tab se · since then

tab = then, tab se = since then

  • Maine Hindi seekhna tab shuru kiya jab main Sajjan Singh Rangroot mein kaam kar raha tha.
    I started learning Hindi when I was working on Sajjan Singh Rangroot.
  • Tab se main Hindi seekh raha hoon.
    Since then I have been learning Hindi.
  • Us film ke baad maine kai aur films mein kaam kiya hai.
    After that film I worked in several more films.

role · director · producer

say what you did, then who made it

  • Maine The Waking of a Nation mein General Dyer ka role kiya hai.
    I played General Dyer in The Waking of a Nation.
  • Maine kai films mein sipahi aur adhikaari ke role kiye hain.
    I have played soldiers and officers in several films.
  • Sajjan Singh Rangroot ke director Pankaj Batra the.
    The director of Sajjan Singh Rangroot was Pankaj Batra.
  • Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga ke producer Dinesh Vijan hain.
    The producer of Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga is Dinesh Vijan.

Patterns · quiz

Small words that are easy to drop

These came up while you were saying your sentences. They are short, so they disappear when you speak fast.

karne ka · karne mein · karne ke liye

after karne, the little word is part of the sentence. Like Lesson 04: karne ka mann karta hai, or karna pasand hai

  • Mujhe aapke saath kaam karne ka bahut mann hai.
    I really want to work with you.
  • Mujhe Hindi bolne mein abhi thodi dikkat hoti hai.
    I still have a little difficulty speaking Hindi.
  • Main mauke dhoondhne ke liye Goa aaya hoon.
    I have come to Goa to look for chances.

aap · karte hain

with aap the verb ends in -te hain, with a nasal hain

  • Aap kya kaam karte hain?
    What work do you do?
  • Aap kahan se hain?
    Where are you from? Said fast, it can sound like Ab kahan se?

kai · kaafi · X hi X

kai is two sounds, ka-ee. Kaafi means quite a lot

  • Maine kai films mein kaam kiya hai.
    I have worked in several films.
  • Maine kaafi Bollywood films aur series mein kaam kiya hai.
    I have worked in quite a lot of Bollywood films and series.
  • Yahan kalakaar hi kalakaar hain.
    There are artists everywhere here.
  • Mujhe kalakaaron se milna bahut pasand hai.
    I really like meeting artists.
  • Yahan bahut dilchasp log hain.
    There are very interesting people here.
  • Yahan kalakaar bhare pade hain.
    This place is full of artists.

Make it sound like a questionRaise your voice at the end: Kya hum contact mein reh sakte hain?

Check yourself

  1. Mujhe aapke saath kaam karne     bahut mann hai.

  2. Mujhe unki soch bahut dilchasp     hai. (soch is feminine)

  3. Aap kya kaam     hain?

  4. Yahan kalakaar     kalakaar hain.

4

Phrases

When they go faster than you

You told me the problem: after you say a practised sentence, people think your Hindi is better than it is. Say it kindly, early, and they will slow down.

  • Meri Hindi abhi itni achhi nahin hai, main seekh raha hoon.

    My Hindi is not that good yet, I am still learning.

  • Thoda dheere boliye.

    Please speak a little slower.

  • Mujhe poori baat samajh nahin aayi.

    I did not understand the whole thing.

  • Main samajh gaya.

    I understood.

5

Speaking

Say it in Hindi

Say your own version first. The hidden versions are models, not scripts you have to copy.

  1. 1

    A director you have just met. You have not seen his work. Show interest in two sentences.

    Compare with an example answer

    Mujhe aapke kaam ke baare mein aur jaanna hai. Aapki kaun si film mujhe pehle dekhni chahiye?

  2. 2

    A producer asks how you started with Hindi. Say who you worked with and when you started.

    Compare with an example answer

    Maine Diljit Dosanjh ke saath Sajjan Singh Rangroot mein kaam kiya hai. Maine Hindi seekhna tab shuru kiya jab main us film mein kaam kar raha tha.

  3. 3

    Say your thought about artists in two sentences.

    Compare with an example answer

    Saare kalakaar chahte hain ki log unhein dekhein aur samjhein. Mujhe kalakaar ka maksad bahut dilchasp lagta hai.

  4. 4

    Someone asks: Festival kaisa lag raha hai? Answer in two or three short sentences.

    Compare with an example answer

    Bahut maza aa raha hai. Mujhe kalakaaron se milna bahut pasand hai. Yahan kalakaar hi kalakaar hain.

  5. 5

    A producer speaks fast and you lose him. Stop him politely, and tell him where you are with Hindi.

    Compare with an example answer

    Maaf kijiye, mujhe poori baat samajh nahin aayi. Thoda dheere boliye. Meri Hindi abhi itni achhi nahin hai, main seekh raha hoon.
