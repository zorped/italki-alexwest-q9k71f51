Phrases

When they say Aap toh Hindi bolte hain!

People who say this are surprised. They are not asking for your whole story, so start short and add more only if they keep talking.

What you will hear

  • Arre, aapko Hindi aati hai?

    Oh, you know Hindi?

  • Arre, aap toh Hindi bolte hain!

    Oh, you actually speak Hindi!

  • Wah, aap itni achhi Hindi kaise bolte hain?

    Wow, how do you speak such good Hindi?

  • Ek angrez aur itni achhi Hindi! Kamaal hai!

    An Englishman with such good Hindi! Amazing!

  • Aapko Hindi kisne sikhayi?

    Who taught you Hindi?

What you can say

  • Haan, main seekh raha hoon.

    Yes, I'm learning. (the short answer)

  • Main acting ke kaam ke liye Hindi seekh raha hoon.

    I'm learning Hindi for my acting work.

  • Maine kuch aise roles kiye hain jinmein Hindi bolni padti thi.

    I've done some roles in which I had to speak Hindi.

  • Ek role mein mujhe Hindi dialogues bolne pade the.

    In one role I had to speak Hindi dialogues.

Short first, then one more sentenceFor a stranger at a party, use the first two. For a director who keeps asking, add the roles. Match your answer to how much they want to know.

Patterns · quiz

Already done, and hard to do

Three patterns from what you said in class. Each one will come up again and again when you talk about your career.

… chuka hoon · already done

chuka (man) · chuki (woman) · chuke (more than one)

  • Main kai Hindi films kar chuka hoon.
    I have already done several Hindi films.
  • Zyaadatar log yeh film dekh chuke hain.
    Most people have already seen this film.

… -na mushkil hai · hard to do

put the verb in the -na form, then the adjective

  • Film ki duniya mein kaamyaabi milna mushkil hai.
    Success in the film world is hard to get.
  • Hindi bolna aasaan nahin hai.
    Speaking Hindi is not easy.

meri dikkat · my difficulty

dikkat is feminine, so meri

  • Meri dikkat yeh hai ki shaam ko mere paas time nahin hota.
    My difficulty is that I do not have time in the evening.

Zyaadatar logZyaadatar log means most people. It is the natural way to say it, and it is shorter than saying a lot of people.

Check yourself

  1. Meri dost yeh film dekh     hai.

  2. Film ki duniya mein kaamyaabi     mushkil hai.

  3.     dikkat yeh hai ki shaam ko mere paas time nahin hota.

4

Speaking

Say it in Hindi

Say your own answer first. The hidden versions are models, not scripts you have to copy.

  1. 1

    Someone says Arre, aap toh Hindi bolte hain! Answer in one sentence. Then answer for a director who wants to know more.

    Compare with an example answer

    Haan, main seekh raha hoon. Main acting ke kaam ke liye Hindi seekh raha hoon. Maine kuch aise roles kiye hain jinmein Hindi bolni padti thi.

  2. 2

    Say two things you have already done in your career. Use chuka hoon.

    Compare with an example answer

    Main kai Hindi films kar chuka hoon. Main ek role mein Hindi dialogues bhi bol chuka hoon.

  3. 3

    Say one of your films, who was in it and who directed it.

    Compare with an example answer

    Maine Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga mein kaam kiya hai. Yami Gautam aur Sunny Kaushal us film mein the. Uske director Ajay Singh hain.
