Phrases

Lines for a director

You wanted a line that says you hope to work with someone, and shows respect without sounding like flattery.

  • Mujhe ummeed hai ki aapke saath kaam karne ka mauka milega.

    I hope I will get a chance to work with you.

  • Meri ummeed hai ki bhavishya mein mujhe aapke saath kaam karne ka mauka mile.

    I hope that in the future I get a chance to work with you. (a little more formal)

  • Meri raay mein aap apne kaam mein maahir hain.

    In my opinion you are very skilled at your work.

  • Main aapke kaam ka bahut aadar karta hoon.

    I have great respect for your work.

Why not “mujhe aapke saath kaam karne ka bahut mann hai”?A director already knows that an actor wants work, so this line says nothing new. Ummeed sounds humbler: you hope, you do not push. Respect also comes from aap and your tone of voice, not from long sentences.

A true line about your Hindi

  • Mujhe Hindi bolne mein abhi bhi thodi dikkat hoti hai, lekin dialogues bolne mein nahin.

    I still find speaking Hindi a little hard, but not speaking dialogues.

  • Jab main dialogues taiyaar karta hoon, mere paas thoda aur samay hota hai.

    When I prepare dialogues, I have a little more time.

Why this line worksIt is honest, and it tells the director something useful: you prepare your dialogues, so you will do them well. You do not need to explain more.

Rules · quiz

Hain or the? Seekhna or seekhne?

Two small rules that came up while you were saying your sentences.

hain, not the

for the person credited with a film

  • Sajjan Singh Rangroot ke director Pankaj Batra hain.
    The director of Sajjan Singh Rangroot is Pankaj Batra.
  • Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga ke producer Dinesh Vijan hain.
    The producer of Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga is Dinesh Vijan.

seekhna or seekhne

-ne only when a small word follows

  • Maine Hindi seekhna tab shuru kiya jab main Sajjan Singh Rangroot mein kaam kar raha tha.
    I started learning Hindi when I was working on Sajjan Singh Rangroot.
  • Mujhe Hindi seekhne ki zaroorat hai.
    I need to learn Hindi.
  • Hindi bolne mein mujhe maza aata hai.
    I enjoy speaking Hindi.

Jab … tabJab and tab work like when and then. Say the tab half first, and the jab half after it, as in the sentence above.

Phrases

More sentences from class

These are the sentences you asked me to say in class, with similar ones you can use in other situations.

kam samay mein … karna tha

had to do a lot in little time

  • Kam samay mein kaafi kaam karna tha.
    We had to do a lot of work in little time.
  • Mujhe kam samay mein bahut saare dialogues yaad karne the.
    I had to learn many dialogues in little time.
  • Hamein kam samay mein kaafi logon se milna tha.
    We had to meet quite a lot of people in little time.

How was it?

asking and answering about an experience

  • Shooting kaisi thi? Ekdum badhiya thi. Bahut maza aaya.
    How was the shoot? It was great. I had a lot of fun.
  • Main mauke dhoondhne ke liye Goa aaya hoon.
    I have come to Goa to look for opportunities.

Small talk about food and weather

useful at any dinner or break

  • Sardiyon mein, jab mausam bahut thanda hota hai, mere liye garam khaana bahut zaroori hota hai.
    In winter, when the weather is very cold, hot food is very important to me.
  • Garam khaana khaane mein bahut maza aata hai.
    Eating hot food is very enjoyable.

Mujhe … karna thaMujhe … karna tha means I had to do it, or I was meant to do it. When the thing is plural, the verb changes with it: dialogues yaad karne the.

Sound · quiz

Maarna or marna?

A long vowel and a short vowel change the meaning, as in kaam and kam in Lesson 01. Say the long aa for longer than you think you need to.

maarna · maara
to hit, or to kill (long aa)Usne machhar ko maara.
marna · mar gaya
to die (short a)Machhar mar gaya.

  • Usne machhar ko maara.

    He killed the mosquito.

  • Machhar mar gaya.

    The mosquito died.

Check yourself

  1. Usne machhar ko    .

  2. Machhar     gaya.

Speaking

Say it in Hindi

Say your own answer first. The hidden versions are models, not scripts you have to copy.

  1. 1

    Say to a director that you hope to work with them, and give one reason. Use ummeed.

    Compare with an example answer

    Mujhe ummeed hai ki aapke saath kaam karne ka mauka milega. Meri raay mein aap apne kaam mein maahir hain.

  2. 2

    Say when you started learning Hindi. Use jab and tab.

    Compare with an example answer

    Maine Hindi seekhna tab shuru kiya jab main ek film mein kaam kar raha tha. Tab se main Hindi seekh raha hoon.

  3. 3

    Tell someone, honestly, what is still hard about your Hindi and what is not.

    Compare with an example answer

    Mujhe Hindi bolne mein abhi bhi thodi dikkat hoti hai, lekin dialogues bolne mein nahin.

  4. 4

    Say what you had to do in little time, and how it went. Use kam samay mein and maza aaya.

    Compare with an example answer

    Mujhe kal kam samay mein bahut saare dialogues yaad karne the, lekin shooting mein bahut maza aaya.
