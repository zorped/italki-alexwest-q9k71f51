Meeting people in film: your first sentences

Eleven short sentences for meeting people in film, and the questions they may ask you. Short on purpose: a clear sentence, said well, is the best first impression.

Start here about 15 minutes

Learn the first three sentences in step 1 until you can say them without looking.

Then say the film titles from Lesson 03 once more. The sentences are for practising out loud, so press ▶ and say each one after the voice.

1

Phrases

Eleven sentences

They go in the order of a real conversation: who you are, what you have done, what you want. All of them use aap, the respectful you, because you are speaking to people you have just met.

Say who you are

  • Namaste, mera naam Alex hai. Main London se hoon aur ek actor hoon.

    Hello, my name is Alex. I am from London and I am an actor.

  • Aapse milkar bahut khushi hui.

    I am very happy to meet you.

  • Maine aapka kaam dekha hai aur mujhe bahut pasand aaya.

    I have seen your work and I liked it a lot.

  • Aajkal aap kis project par kaam kar rahe hain?

    What project are you working on these days?

Say what you have done

  • Maine kai films aur series mein kaam kiya hai.

    I have worked in several films and series.

  • Main Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga aur Mission Raniganj jaisi films mein kaam kar chuka hoon.

    I have already worked in films like Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga and Mission Raniganj.

  • Maine acting seekhi hai.

    I have trained as an actor. (Add where: Maine ___ mein acting seekhi hai.)

Say what you want and what you think

  • Mujhe Hindi cinema mein kaam karne ka bahut mann karta hai.

    I really want to work in Hindi cinema.

  • Main Hindi films mein kaam karne ke mauke dhoondh raha hoon.

    I am looking for chances to work in Hindi films.

  • Mujhe yeh kahani bahut dilchasp lagti hai.

    I find this story very interesting.

  • Aapse baat karke bahut achha laga. Kya hum contact mein reh sakte hain?

    It was very good to talk to you. Can we stay in contact?

Make them yoursChange the film titles to fit the person: use the film you did with that producer or director. When you are sure where you trained, say it in one more sentence: Maine ___ mein acting seekhi hai.

2

Listen · answer

Questions you may hear

Listen to the question first and answer it yourself. Then press ▶ on the answer to hear a model at your level. The answers are short, because a short answer lets the conversation move on.

  • Aap kahan se hain?
    Where are you from?
  • Main England se hoon. Main London mein rehta hoon.
    I am from England. I live in London.
  • Aap kya kaam karte hain?
    What work do you do?
  • Main actor hoon.
    I am an actor.
  • Aapne Hindi kahan seekhi?
    Where did you learn Hindi?
  • Main acting ke kaam ke liye Hindi seekh raha hoon.
    I am learning Hindi for my acting work.
  • Aap kab se Hindi seekh rahe hain?
    Since when have you been learning Hindi?
  • Main kuch mahine se Hindi seekh raha hoon.
    I have been learning Hindi for a few months. (Change the time to fit you.)
  • Aapne pehle kaun si films ki hain?
    Which films have you done before?
  • Maine Mission Raniganj aur Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga mein kaam kiya hai.
    I have worked in Mission Raniganj and Chor Nikal Ke Bhaaga.
  • Aapko kis tarah ke roles pasand hain?
    What kind of roles do you like?
  • Mujhe aise roles pasand hain jinmein mujhe character ko samajhna padta hai.
    I like roles in which I have to understand the character.
  • Festival kaisa lag raha hai?
    How are you finding the festival?
  • Mujhe bahut achha lag raha hai. Yahan bahut achhi films hain.
    I am enjoying it a lot. There are many good films here.
  • Aapki Hindi bahut achhi hai!
    Your Hindi is very good!
  • Shukriya! Abhi main seekh raha hoon.
    Thank you! I am still learning.

If you do not understandSay: Maaf kijiye, kya aap dobara bol sakte hain? (Sorry, could you say that again?) It is polite, and it is better than guessing.

Phrases · quiz

First impressions last

Hindi does not have one fixed saying for this, so here are three natural ways to say it. You can use any of them when you explain why you speak Hindi to people in the industry.

  • Pehla impression bahut matlab rakhta hai.

    The first impression matters a lot. (the everyday way)

  • Pehli mulaaqaat ka asar der tak rehta hai.

    The effect of the first meeting stays for a long time. (closest to “first impressions last”)

  • Pehli mulaaqaat mein achhi chhaap chhodna zaroori hai.

    It is important to leave a good impression in the first meeting.

Speaking

Say it in Hindi

Say your own version first. The hidden versions are models, not scripts you have to copy.

  1. 1

    You meet a director. Say who you are in three short sentences.

    Compare with an example answer

    Namaste, mera naam Alex hai. Main London se hoon aur ek actor hoon. Aapse milkar bahut khushi hui.

  2. 2

    Tell a producer you have seen his work, and ask what he is working on now.

    Compare with an example answer

    Maine aapka kaam dekha hai aur mujhe bahut pasand aaya. Aajkal aap kis project par kaam kar rahe hain?

  3. 3

    A casting director asks: Aapne pehle kaun si films ki hain? and then Aapne Hindi kahan seekhi? Answer both.

    Compare with an example answer

    Maine Mission Raniganj mein kaam kiya hai. Main acting ke kaam ke liye Hindi seekh raha hoon.

  4. 4

    The conversation is ending. Say what you want, and ask to stay in contact.

    Compare with an example answer

    Mujhe Hindi cinema mein kaam karne ka bahut mann karta hai. Aapse baat karke bahut achha laga. Kya hum contact mein reh sakte hain?
